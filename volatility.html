Coin filters (top N, meme floor, new listings) apply on the next Refresh. Trigger sliders apply right away.

How it works: every refresh pulls 5-minute and 1-hour candles for each coin from OKX. Volume in the window is compared with that coin's median volume (15m vs the last 24h, 1h and 4h vs the last 7 days), so one old spike doesn't skew "normal". ×N = how many times the coin hit the same trigger in the look-back (4h for ⚡ and 🔥, 12h for 🌊). Skipped: stocks/commodity perps, stablecoins, coins listed <14 days ago, memes under the meme floor.

This is a scanner, not a strategy. It is not backtested. A volume spike says something is happening, not which way it goes next. Check BTC and the chart before acting, size live trades at 0.5–1% risk, and don't chase a candle that already ran. Not financial advice.
