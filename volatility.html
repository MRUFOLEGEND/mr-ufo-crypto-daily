How to use this (tap)
  1. Longs come from the Leaders. Only when BTC's daily trend is up or flat. The best ones show RS 30d high and Holds red days and are not Extended. Prefer coins above all three daily EMAs (▲20 ▲50 ▲200, same as the Crypto Daily overview). Buy pullbacks toward the 20 EMA, not green candles.
  2. Shorts come from the Laggards. Only when BTC is in a downtrend. Best: RS 30d low + Bleeds red days + below all three daily EMAs (▼20 ▼50 ▼200).
  3. Red-day edge is the real test. It's the coin's average daily result minus BTC's on days BTC closed red (last 30 days). A coin that holds up while BTC dumps is usually first to run when BTC turns.
  4. Watch the beta. High beta coins (≥2) outperform in every rally just by moving harder. If the red-day edge is negative, that outperformance is leverage, not strength.
  5. Breadth sets the mode. Over 60% of alts beating BTC = alt-friendly market. Under 30% = BTC dominance phase: trade only the top leaders, or BTC itself.

Settings

Coin filters (top N, meme floor, new listings) apply on the next Refresh. Trigger sliders apply right away.

How it works: every refresh pulls 5-minute and 1-hour candles for each coin from OKX. Volume in the window is compared with that coin's median volume (15m vs the last 24h, 1h and 4h vs the last 7 days), so one old spike doesn't skew "normal". ×N = how many times the coin hit the same trigger in the look-back (4h for ⚡ and 🔥, 12h for 🌊). Skipped: stocks/commodity perps, stablecoins, coins listed <14 days ago, memes under the meme floor.

Relative strength: 300 daily candles (UTC) for the same top-N list; EMA 20/50/200 on the daily close ("–" = coin too new for that EMA), refreshed every 60 min, with live prices on every Refresh. RS rating 1–99 = percentile blend of performance vs BTC over 1d (20%), 7d (40%) and 30d (40%).

This is a scanner, not a strategy. It is not backtested. A volume spike says something is happening, not which way it goes next. Check BTC and the chart before acting, size live trades at 0.5–1% risk, and don't chase a candle that already ran. Not financial advice.
