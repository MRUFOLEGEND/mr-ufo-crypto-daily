A · Break & RetestCore
  1. Mark the level: the range high the coin broke from.
  2. A candle closes through it on above-average volume, after a slow grind into the level rather than one spike.
  3. Price comes back to the level on lower volume.
  4. Enter on a rejection candle. Stop goes below the retest low minus 0.5 ATR.
B · 8/21 PullbackTrend
  1. Daily 8 EMA over the 21: buyers are in control.
  2. Price dips into the 4H 21 EMA or the daily 8 EMA, above the last swing low.
  3. Enter on the first 4H close back above the 4H 8 EMA.
  4. Stop goes below the dip low minus 0.5 × 4H ATR. T1 is the prior high.
C · Pending BreakWatch
  1. The coin is pressing a level but hasn't closed through it.
  2. No order yet. Set an alert at the level.
  3. After a daily close through it, the coin becomes a Setup A trade.
  4. A spike through the level and back inside is a rejection. Skip it.
