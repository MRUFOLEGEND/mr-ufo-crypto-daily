How each check is measured

Wedge: two trendlines through swing points, both sloping the same way and converging, at least 4 touches, 10–80 candles, no close outside. Same detector as the backtest. Only closed candles are used.

1 · Order block (4H): supply = last green 4H candle before a drop of at least 2 ATR within 3 candles that closes below the last swing low. Demand is the mirror. Zone = that candle's low to high. Dead once a 4H candle closes through it. Fresh = not touched between its creation and the start of the wedge. The wedge's high (low for falling) must be within 0.5 4H ATR of the zone.

2 · Volume: last third of the wedge under 0.8× the first third. A triggered wedge also needs a break candle at 1.5× the wedge average.

3 · Sweep: a wick beyond the outer line by more than 0.1 ATR that closed back inside, in the last third of the wedge.

4 · Divergence: RSI(14) lower at the last swing high than at the one before, while price is higher (mirror for falling).

5 · Derivatives: open interest up more than 3% over 24h, funding positive for a short idea and negative for a long idea. Only fetched for coins at a fresh order block.

6 · Context: coin's 4H EMA50 below EMA200 for a short idea (above for long), and BTC 4H close on the same side of its EMA50.

Plan: trigger = next value of the trigger line. Stop = beyond the wedge extreme or the order-block edge, whichever is further, plus 0.25 ATR. Target 1 = 2R. "R to zone" = distance to the nearest opposite live 4H order block in R. Under 2R means skip.

A GitHub workflow rescans all coins every 15 minutes. Refresh loads the newest scan; it does not start a new one. Wedges alone were backtested with no edge; the order-block filter is not backtested. Keep live risk at 0.5–1% per trade. Not financial advice.
